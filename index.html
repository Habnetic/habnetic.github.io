About Habnetic

Open research for uncertainty-aware urban decisions

Habnetic is an independent open-source research project developing Bayesian methods for posterior decision stability and robust prioritisation under uncertainty.

Current work focuses on urban flood prioritisation, using Rotterdam as the baseline case followed by cross-city transfer to Hamburg and Donostia.

  • Bayesian inference with PyMC
  • Posterior predictive checks
  • Cross-city transfer
  • Open data and reproducible code
